Accounting and Tax Services

We keep your accounting up to date and help you comply with all your tax obligations.

  • Monthly or periodic accounting;
  • Accounting records and bank reconciliations;
  • Annual tax closing;
  • Filing of VAT, withholdings, dividends;
  • Income certifications and other financial reports;
  • Advisory on your business’s accounting structure;
  • Preparation of digital accounting books.

Income Tax and Municipal Declarations

We help you prepare and file your income tax returns and municipal contributions correctly and on time.

  • Tax return advisory;
  • Review of deductions and tax credits;
  • Online filing with the corresponding municipality.

Payroll and Labor Management

We manage your company’s payroll and ensure compliance with all labor and social security obligations.

  • Preparation of monthly payrolls;
  • Payment of social benefits and allowances;
  • Management of employee contracts and insurance.

Company Incorporation and Formalization

We guide you through the entire process of creating your company, from planning to legal formalization.

  • Legal registration of the company;
  • Obtaining licenses and permits;
  • Advisory on corporate structure and bylaws.

Tax Refunds

We manage tax refunds efficiently, ensuring that you receive the amount you are entitled to.

  • Review of overpayments;
  • Procedures before the tax authority;
  • Preparation of required documentation.

External Audits

We conduct external audits to assess the financial situation and ensure your company’s transparency.

  • Annual financial audit;
  • Evaluation of internal processes;
  • Detailed report with recommendations.

Other Procedures

We assist you with various administrative and legal procedures your business may need.

  • Renewal of licenses and permits;
  • Procedures before public entities;
  • Management of additional legal documents.
